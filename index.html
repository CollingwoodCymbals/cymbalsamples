Alloy. A free cymbal plugin with real hand-hammered hits.

I make cymbals by hand in my Bristol workshop. Alloy is a free plugin that plays recordings of them inside music software such as Logic, Cubase, Reaper or Ableton. Every hit is recorded on a stereo ribbon pair and a mono mic, and one fader blends between the two.

  • VST3 for Windows and Mac, plus AU for Logic
  • Level, compression, punch, drive and a four-band EQ on each mic
  • Comes with my first set of example hits, all free
  • You can try it in your browser first, below

How to install

Windows

  1. Unzip the download. You'll get a folder called Alloy.vst3.
  2. Move it into your VST3 folder. The usual one is C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
  3. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW and add Alloy to an instrument track.

Mac

  1. Unzip the download and open READ ME FIRST.txt.
  2. In Finder choose Go › Go to Folder… and paste ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components, then drag Alloy.component in. For VST3 hosts, put Alloy.vst3 in ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3.
  3. In Logic, make a Software Instrument track and pick AU Instruments › Collingwood › Alloy.

Loading the hits

  1. Unzip the hits. Keep the folder Collingwood - Alloy Test 1 together (it holds Ribbon, Mono and alloy.txt).
  2. In Alloy press LOAD.
  3. Go to the folder Collingwood - Alloy Test 1 and open it. You'll see Ribbon, Mono and alloy.txt.
  4. Don't go into Ribbon or Mono. Click Load there and Alloy loads both mics and the alignment in one go.
  5. Or drag the Collingwood - Alloy Test 1 folder onto Alloy.
  6. Play C4 (MIDI note 60) for the tip.
The example hits

How Test 1 was recorded

My 21¾" Dark Ride, recorded in my workshop in Bristol.

Cymbal21¾" Dark Ride, 2406 g, hand-hammered by me
ArticulationTip, played with a Vic Firth Steve Jordan stick
Hits20: 5 velocity layers × 4 round robins
Stereo pair2 × Alder Audio ALDER ribbon mics as a Blumlein (crossed figure-8) pair, about 80 cm away
Mono micJZ Vintage V11, about 35 cm above the bow, halfway between bell and edge, angled slightly towards the bell
InterfaceFocusrite Scarlett 18i20 (3rd gen)
Format48 kHz, 24-bit WAV. Both setups recorded at the same moment
Mic timingThe mono mic hears each hit 0.68 ms before the ribbons. Alloy's ALIGN lines them up
EditingCut just before each hit, short fade in, rings out to silence. One gain per mic, so the layers keep their real dynamics
ProcessingNone, apart from a 150 Hz low-cut during recording
