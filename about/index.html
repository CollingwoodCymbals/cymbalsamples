About Dave Collingwood

I make cymbals by hand in my workshop in Bristol, UK. I taught myself, and for years I've helped other makers teach themselves.

  • Makers have come to my workshop from Spain, Germany, Israel, France, Belgium and South Africa.
  • I've travelled to teach in the US, Hawaii, Norway and Belgium.
  • Many of the makers I've taught now run their own cymbal companies.

The plug-ins and samples on this site come out of that work. Making cymbals and teaching other people to make them is where most of my ideas start.

Every sound here is recorded from my own hand-hammered cymbals.

Handmade cymbals and commissions: collingwoodcymbals.com

Make your own cymbal with me in Bristol: makeyourowncymbal.com
