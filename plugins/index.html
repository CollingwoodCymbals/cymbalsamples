Mono

One big button: stereo or mono, nothing else.

Phones, laptops, club systems and Bluetooth speakers all fold your mix to mono sooner or later. Mono lets you hear that with one click, so you can catch anything that disappears.

Press it and both sides play the same signal. Press it again and your audio comes back untouched, bit for bit. Every switch fades over 10 ms, so it never clicks, even mid-playback or under automation.

  • One-click STEREO / MONO with a click-free 10 ms fade
  • Mono is (L + R) / 2 on both sides; stereo is untouched, bit for bit
  • Zero latency, almost no CPU
  • Automatable
  • Sharp on Retina and high-DPI screens

How to install

Windows

  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Copy the Mono.vst3 folder into your VST3 folder. The usual one is C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
  3. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW. Mono is listed under Collingwood Cymbals.

Mac

  1. Unzip the download.
  2. In Finder choose Go › Go to Folder… and paste ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components, then drag Mono.component in. For VST3 hosts, put Mono.vst3 in ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3.
  3. Open your DAW. Mono is listed under Collingwood Cymbals.
