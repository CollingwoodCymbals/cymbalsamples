Strata

A delay that only echoes the frequencies you choose.

Draw up to five bands on the curve to pick out parts of the sound. Each band gets its own echo time, feedback and pan, and everything else stays dry. Your original signal is never filtered.

  • Up to 5 bands: bell, low shelf or high shelf
  • Each band has its own echo time (1 ms to 4 s, or synced from 1/64 to 2 bars, straight, dotted or triplet), feedback, pan, ping-pong and pre-delay
  • Amount up to 150 % per band
  • Drive In, and Drive Loop that gets grittier with every repeat
  • Lo-Fi switch for worn, tape-style repeats
  • Live analyser behind the curve, and zoom down to a single octave
  • Zero latency, low CPU, fully automatable
  • Tells you when an update is out

Strata, Mono and Alloy are free. If they're useful to you, donations are gratefully received.

How to install

Windows

  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Copy the Strata.vst3 folder into your VST3 folder. The usual one is C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
  3. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW. Strata is listed under Collingwood Cymbals.

Mac

  1. Unzip the download.
  2. In Finder choose Go › Go to Folder… and paste ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components, then drag Strata.component in. For VST3 hosts, put Strata.vst3 in ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3.
  3. Open your DAW. In Logic it's under Audio Units › Collingwood › Strata.

Mono

One big button: stereo or mono, nothing else.

Phones, laptops, club systems and Bluetooth speakers all fold your mix to mono sooner or later. Mono lets you hear that with one click, so you can catch anything that disappears.

Press it and both sides play the same signal. Press it again and your audio comes back untouched, bit for bit. Every switch fades over 10 ms, so it never clicks, even mid-playback or under automation.

  • One-click STEREO / MONO with a click-free 10 ms fade
  • Mono is (L + R) / 2 on both sides; stereo is untouched, bit for bit
  • Zero latency, almost no CPU
  • Automatable
  • Sharp on Retina and high-DPI screens
