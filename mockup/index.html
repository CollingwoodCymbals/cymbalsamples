How they're made

Two recordings of every hit

Every single sample is captured on two setups at the same moment, so they line up perfectly and blend without phasing.

1

Stereo ribbon pair

Smooth, wide and natural. The cymbal as you hear it standing in front of the kit.

2

Mono close mic

Focused and detailed, easy to sit in a busy mix or pan wherever you like.

3

Edited by hand

Every hit trimmed, faded to true silence and named consistently. No normalising that ruins the velocity layers.

Questions

FAQ

How do I get my files?

You get a download link straight after paying, and by email. Links stay in your account so you can download again later.

Can I use them in released music?

Yes. Use them in your own tracks, commercially or not. You just can't resell or share the samples themselves.

Do I need a special sampler?

No. They're plain WAV files, so any DAW or sampler works. Alloy, my free player, is just an optional extra if you want an easy way to blend the two mics.

Is there an MP3 version?

Not yet. WAV only for now.

Can I buy the actual cymbal?

Many packs are of cymbals that are still available. Each pack page links to the cymbal if it is.
